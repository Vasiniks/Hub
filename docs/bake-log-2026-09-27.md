## Blocker found — the lightmaps cannot be mapped at runtime as-is

Not repaired here (handoff rule); baked and committed at the owner's instruction for review.

1. **Repacked UVs are discarded.** `ensure_lightmap_uv()` runs `bpy.ops.uv.lightmap_pack(...,
   PREF_NEW_UVLAYER=False)` on each mesh's `Lightmap` layer, overwriting the UVs authored in
   `assets/processed/*.glb`, and bakes into that new layout. Nothing exports it (no GLB export, no
   `.blend` save, no UV dump), so it is lost when Blender exits. The runtime reads the GLB's
   original `Lightmap` UVs, so these atlases will not line up. The atlases confirm the repack:
   they are `lightmap_pack` triangle-pair mosaics (chair/desk: thousands of 5–10 px triangles).
2. **Probable (not verified): multi-mesh groups overlap.** Each mesh is packed on its own into the
   full 0–1 tile, then all meshes of a group bake into one image, so islands likely stack and
   overwrite. 15 of 19 groups have >1 mesh. Consistent with very low atlas coverage for keyboard
   (2.5 %), organizer (3.0 %), cable (0.9 %).

Fix options for the owning machine: bake into the authored `Lightmap` UV without repacking, or
export the repacked UVs alongside the bake (and pack a group's meshes together).

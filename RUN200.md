## Calibration note (do not regress)

Three measurement methods were tried and two lie on this stack:
  - `gl.finish()` does not block on ANGLE/Metal (reported 1.4ms for a 21ms frame).
  - rAF frame deltas lie while the loading screen is up: nothing is being presented, so rAF
    free-runs at the refresh rate while the driver queues a backlog.
Only a `fenceSync` + `clientWaitSync` burst tells the truth. Calibration also has to drive
`step()`, not `composer.render()` — the render is a whole vsync interval cheaper than a frame.
